How we choose

Buy It Smart helps you buy the right product the first time. Every guide starts with the criteria that change your daily life, then gives one pick per budget.

Our method

  • Criteria first. We explain what to look for before naming any model.
  • Expert tests & reviews. We cross-check published expert tests and the recurring complaints of verified buyers.
  • One pick per budget. Small, medium and premium, so everyone finds an option.
  • Honest limits. We tell you what a product does not do. Specs and prices change: always check the product page.
