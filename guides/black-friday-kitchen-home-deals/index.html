How to spot a real Black Friday deal: 6 checks

01

Check the price history, not the “was” price

A big crossed-out price is not proof of a deal. Look at the last 90 days of prices with a free price tracker (Keepa or CamelCamelCamel, as a browser extension or website). A real Black Friday deal is close to the lowest price of the year, not just lower than an inflated “list price”.

💡 Set a price alert now: you get an email the moment the price drops, even before Black Friday.
02

Compare the exact model number

Brands often sell several versions that look alike: a different capacity, an older generation, or a version without the self-emptying dock. Copy the full model name from the product page and compare it with the one in our guides before you decide the price is good.

💡 For robot vacuums, check what is in the box: dock, mop pads, spare brushes and bags.
03

Know what you need before the sale

Deals push you toward the bigger, cheaper-looking model. Decide first: how many people you cook for (air fryer size), and your floors, pets and home size (robot vacuum). Our two buying guides take 5 minutes and give you the right type.

💡 Write down your top 3 must-have features and your maximum price.
04

Count the running costs

A cheap robot vacuum can cost more over time if its bags, filters and brushes are expensive or hard to find. For air fryers, check the price of a replacement basket or crisper plate.

💡 Search the model name + “replacement bags” or “replacement basket” on Amazon before you buy.
05

Check who sells it and the return window

Prefer items shipped and sold by Amazon or by the brand’s official store. Check the return date shown at checkout: Amazon has offered extended returns on holiday purchases in recent years, which helps for gifts.

💡 Keep the box until the return date if it is a gift.
06

Don’t wait for the last minute

Popular deals sell out, and many Black Friday prices start days before the Friday. If the price you wanted shows up early, it is usually smarter to take it than to wait for a lower one that may never come.

Black Friday FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday 2026 is on Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26). Cyber Monday is on Monday, November 30, 2026.

When does Amazon’s Black Friday sale start in 2026?

As of early November 2026, Amazon has not announced its Black Friday dates. In recent years its Black Friday sale started several days before the Friday. We will update this guide as soon as the dates are official.

Is Black Friday the cheapest time to buy an air fryer or a robot vacuum?

Often, but not always: prices can be just as low during Prime Day or other sales. The only reliable test is the price history of the exact model: if today’s price is at or near its lowest of the last 90 days, it is a good deal.

What is a good kitchen gift for mom or for new homeowners?

A basket air fryer is one of the most used kitchen gifts: the COSORI Pro LE 5-Qt for a family, the Ninja Crispi glass air fryer for a healthy cook or a small kitchen. For new homeowners, a robot vacuum like the Roborock Q10 S5+ saves time every day.

Are special “Black Friday” models worse than regular ones?

Sometimes stores sell versions with small differences (smaller capacity, fewer accessories). Compare the exact model number with the one in a trusted review before you buy.
