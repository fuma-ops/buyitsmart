What matters most for non-toxic, PFAS-free cooking

01

Check every part that touches food

Basket, crisper plate, inner walls: a “PFAS-free” label may cover only some of them.

02

Glass, steel or ceramic

Glass and stainless steel have no coating; ceramic is a coating but not a traditional PTFE one.

03

Care for any coating

Never use metal utensils or scouring pads, and replace a basket if the coating peels.

FAQ

Are air fryers toxic?

Nonstick coatings are generally considered safe when they are not scratched or overheated. If you prefer to avoid them, choose glass, stainless steel or ceramic surfaces.

What does PFAS-free mean on an air fryer?

It is the brand’s own claim about the materials used. Check which parts it covers on the product page.
