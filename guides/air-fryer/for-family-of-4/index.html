02

Dual drawer or oven?

A dual drawer cooks a main and a side together; an oven-style air fryer bakes and roasts on flat trays.

FAQ

What size air fryer for a family of 4?

About 5 quarts for everyday cooking, or 6 quarts and up, or an air fryer oven, if you batch cook or cook a whole chicken.

Is a dual basket air fryer good for a family?

Yes if you often cook a main and a side at the same time. Otherwise a single large basket is simpler to clean.
