How we calculate it

Food has to sit in one layer for the hot air to crisp it. Our rule of thumb: about 2 to 3 quarts for one person, 4 to 5 quarts for two to four people, and 6 quarts or more, or an air fryer oven, for families and batch cooking. At the same capacity, a square basket gives more flat space than a round one.
