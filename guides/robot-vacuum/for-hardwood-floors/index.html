What matters most for hardwood floors

01

Mid-range suction is enough

Hard floors don’t trap dirt like carpet. Spend on navigation and brushes rather than the highest Pa number.

02

Neat rows with LiDAR

LiDAR mapping cleans in straight lines and doesn’t miss spots along walls and under tables.

03

Mop with little water

Wood doesn’t like standing water: a robot mop that dampens rather than soaks is safer. Follow your floor maker’s advice.

04

A mop that lifts on rugs

If you have rugs on your hardwood, the mop should lift or the robot should avoid them.

FAQ

Will a robot vacuum scratch hardwood floors?

Normally not: wheels and brushes are designed for hard floors. Keep the wheels and brush free of grit, which is what can mark a floor.

Can I use a robot mop on hardwood?

Yes on sealed hardwood, with little water. Check your floor maker’s cleaning advice first.
