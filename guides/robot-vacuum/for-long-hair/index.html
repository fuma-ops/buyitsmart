FAQ

Which robot vacuum brushes are best for long hair?

Dual rubber or anti-tangle brushes. Classic bristle brushes catch and wrap long hair much more often.

How do I get hair off a robot vacuum brush?

Remove the brush, then cut or pull the hair along the groove that many brushes have for this. Anti-tangle designs need this far less often.
