What matters most for vacuuming and mopping

01

How it mops

Vibrating pads, spinning pads and roller mops don’t clean the same way: rollers that rinse themselves avoid spreading dirty water.

02

A mop that lifts on carpet

A mop that stays down will wet your rugs. Choose one that lifts its mop or can avoid carpets.

03

Washing and drying

A station that washes and dries the mop reduces odors and maintenance.

04

Check the running costs

Mop pads, filters and cleaning solution: check their price and availability before you buy.

FAQ

Do robot vacuum and mop combos really clean?

They are great for daily dust and light dirt on hard floors. Sticky stains still need a manual mop now and then.

Will a mopping robot wet my rugs?

Not if it lifts its mop on carpet or if you set no-mop zones. Check this before you buy.
