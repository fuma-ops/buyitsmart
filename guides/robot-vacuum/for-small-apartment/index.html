What matters most for small apartments

Room mapping and no-go zones

Keep the robot away from the pet bowl, the cables behind the TV or the kids’ corner.

02

Check the dock footprint

Self-emptying and mopping docks need space around them: measure the spot before you buy.

03

Height under your furniture

Measure the gap under your sofa and bed and compare it with the robot’s height on the product page.

Noise matters in small spaces

FAQ

Is a robot vacuum worth it for a small apartment?

Yes for most people: in a small space it can run every day in a few minutes, and a self-emptying dock means almost no maintenance.

Do I need a mopping robot in a small apartment?

It is a bonus for kitchens and bathrooms with hard floors. Choose a mop that lifts on carpets if you have rugs.
